\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented MSTNet, a multi-frequency Transformer that uses Sentinel-2 time series, daily Xavier climate, and MapBiomas Solo covariates to predict soybean productivity from municipal PAM labels while scoring soy pixels for sub-municipal maps.
Under year leave-one-out on Paran\'{a} harvest years 2020--2024, municipal aggregates of pixel scores reach mean $R^2=0.302$ and RMSE $=0.427$\,\si{t/ha} when the 2022 drought fold is excluded; that drought LOO fold fails ($R^2=-0.422$) because no similar season remains in training.

These figures give a reproducible LOO benchmark for municipal-supervision, pixel-scoring yield models in Paran\'{a}.
Calibrated intramunicipal maps, broader geographies, and claims about individual covariate groups still need field checks, matched ablations, and additional LOO seasons.
