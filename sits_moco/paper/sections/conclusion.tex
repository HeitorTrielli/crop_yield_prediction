\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented MSTNet, a multi-frequency Transformer that uses Sentinel-2 time series, daily Xavier climate, and MapBiomas Solo covariates to predict soybean productivity from municipal PAM labels while scoring soy pixels for sub-municipal maps.
On the 2021 municipality holdout for Paran\'{a} (harvest years 2020--2024), municipal aggregates at the selected epoch reach $R^2=0.424$ and RMSE $=0.313$\,\si{t/ha} on validation, and $R^2=0.398$ and RMSE $=0.309$\,\si{t/ha} on test.

These figures give a reproducible municipal benchmark for the setting we study.
Calibrated intramunicipal maps and claims about the value of each covariate group still need field checks, matched ablations, and multi-year holdouts.
