\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented MSTNet, a multi-frequency Transformer that learns soybean productivity from municipal PAM labels and scores soy pixels for sub-municipal maps.
Sentinel-2 time series and daily Xavier climate enter parallel STNet trunks; MapBiomas Solo enters the decoder by late fusion.
Training uses soy-masked municipal means; after training, the same weights score every soy pixel and average to municipalities or arbitrary polygons.

Under year leave-one-out on Paran\'{a} harvest years 2020--2024, excluding the 2022 drought fold, municipal-mean LOO reaches mean $R^2=0.275$ and the pixel path reaches mean $R^2=0.302$ with RMSE $=0.427$\,\si{t/ha}.
The 2022 fold fails under both paths because no similar drought season remains in training.
That failure is a coverage problem: training sets that include poor-yield municipality--years from other seasons and regions should make drought-like responses recognizable.
Modality ablations show that climate contributes the most on non-drought folds, with Sentinel-2 next and soil last, while intramunicipal maps remain qualitative until field yields are available to score them.
