\section*{Data availability}
Municipal productivity labels are from IBGE PAM and provide the only supervised signal.
Sentinel-2 L2A reflectance was processed in Google Earth Engine with MapBiomas soybean masking; daily Xavier climate and MapBiomas Solo enter as described in Section~\ref{sec:data}.
Training uses municipality-mean Sentinel-2 series; reported evaluation scores soy pixels and averages to municipalities (Sections~\ref{sec:methods} and~\ref{app:pixel-pipeline}).
Further details are available from the authors upon reasonable request.
