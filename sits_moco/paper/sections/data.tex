\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

The analysis uses municipal PAM productivity labels together with Sentinel-2, daily climate, and soil covariates (Table~\ref{tab:data-sources}).
Soy-masked Sentinel-2 pixels provide the spectral signal. Climate is reduced to a municipal daily series. Soil can be attached per pixel or as a municipal vector.
Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow} shows the municipality-mean spectral path used for training.
Appendix~\ref{app:pixel-pipeline} shows the pixel-by-pixel path used for the reported LOO metrics and for sub-municipal maps.

\subsection{Study area and labels}
The study covers soybean-producing municipalities in the state of Paran\'{a}, Brazil.
The target is municipal soybean productivity in tonnes per hectare (\si{t/ha}) from IBGE PAM~\citep{ibgepam}; municipality code and harvest year identify each observation.
No field- or pixel-level yield labels are used.
We retain municipality--year pairs whose Sentinel-2 coverage ratio relative to the reported planted area lies in $[0.5, 1.5]$, so that satellite coverage is roughly consistent with official area statistics.

\subsection{Covariates and municipal aggregation}
Sentinel-2 L2A reflectance is processed in Google Earth Engine~\citep{gorelick2017gee}: for each municipality and acquisition date we compute soy-masked zonal means of ten bands (B2, B3, B4, B5, B6, B7, B8, B8A, B11, B12), using MapBiomas Collection~10 class~39 as the soybean mask~\citep{mapbiomas2025}.
Each municipal spectral sequence carries a day-of-year index for positional encoding and is truncated or padded to $T=45$ acquisitions.
Climate covariates come from the Xavier daily weather grid~\citep{xavier2022} as a separate municipal daily series over the inclusive Oct~1--Mar~31 window (length $183$): precipitation, reference evapotranspiration (ETo), solar radiation (Rs), maximum temperature (Tmax), and minimum temperature (Tmin).
Daily climate is averaged over Xavier cells whose centers fall in the municipality polygon and is scaled independently of reflectance; MSTNet encodes it in a dedicated trunk rather than concatenating climate onto Sentinel-2 timesteps.
Static MapBiomas Solo properties (clay, silt, sand, and SOC stock at 0--30\,cm) are late-fused in the decoder~\citep{mapbiomassoil2025}.
An ablation omits climate and soil inputs and uses the ten spectral bands alone.

\subsection{Year leave-one-out splits}
Evaluation follows year leave-one-out over harvest years $\{2020,2021,2022,2023,2024\}$ (Section~\ref{sec:loo}).
In each fold, every municipality--year from the four training years is used for fitting; the left-out year is never seen in training and provides the only reported municipal scores for that fold.
Target standardization is recomputed from each fold's training years.
All main-text metrics are municipal aggregates obtained by averaging pixel-level scores on the LOO year only (Section~\ref{sec:pixel-formulation}).
We do not validate pixel maps against field yields.
