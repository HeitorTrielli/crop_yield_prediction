\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

The analysis uses municipal PAM productivity labels together with Sentinel-2, daily climate, and soil covariates (Table~\ref{tab:data-sources}).
All three covariate streams are gridded; for municipal aggregates we average them under the MapBiomas soybean mask.
Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow} shows the municipality-mean path used for training and for the municipal-mean LOO metrics in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}.
After training we also run a pixel-wise forward pass and average the scores for the pixel-path LOO metrics and sub-municipal maps in Section~\ref{sec:results}; Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow-pixel} diagrams that pixel path.

\subsection{Study area and labels}
The study covers soybean-producing municipalities in the state of Paran\'{a}, Brazil.
The target is municipal soybean productivity in tonnes per hectare (\si{t/ha}) from IBGE PAM~\citep{ibgepam}; municipality code and harvest year identify each observation.
No field- or pixel-level yield labels are used.
For each municipality--year we compare the MapBiomas soybean area with the PAM planted area and keep only pairs whose ratio lies in $[0.5, 1.5]$.
This drops cases where the mask covers far less or far more soy than PAM reports, so the soy-masked covariates are not badly mismatched to the official area.

\subsection{Covariates and municipal aggregation}
Both Sentinel-2 imagery and daily climate covariates cover the same inclusive Oct~1--Mar~31 window. We use this fixed window as an approximation of the soybean growing season in Paran\'{a}, assuming sowing begins around Oct~1 and harvest is completed by Mar~31.
Sentinel-2 L2A reflectance is processed in Google Earth Engine~\citep{gorelick2017gee}: for each municipality and observation date we compute soy-masked zonal means of ten bands (B2, B3, B4, B5, B6, B7, B8, B8A, B11, B12), using MapBiomas Collection~10 class~39 as the soybean mask~\citep{mapbiomas2025}.
Each municipal spectral sequence retains a day-of-year index, and the longest observed sequence contains 45 observations.
Climate covariates come from the Xavier daily weather grid~\citep{xavier2022}: precipitation, reference evapotranspiration (ETo), solar radiation (Rs), maximum temperature (Tmax), and minimum temperature (Tmin).
The window contains 182 daily observations in ordinary seasons and 183 when February has 29 days.
As with reflectance, daily climate is averaged under the MapBiomas soybean mask and scaled independently of Sentinel-2; MSTNet encodes it in a dedicated trunk rather than concatenating climate onto Sentinel-2 timesteps.
For each municipality--season, we construct a four-value MapBiomas Solo soil vector comprising static clay, silt, and sand fractions~\citep{mapbiomassoil2025} and the annual SOC stock for the corresponding harvest year~\citep{mapbiomassoc2025}, all at 0--30\,cm. These properties are likewise soy-masked, and the resulting per-season vector is late-fused in the decoder.

\subsection{Year leave-one-out splits}
Evaluation follows year leave-one-out over harvest years 2020--2024 (Section~\ref{sec:loo}).
In each fold, every municipality--year from the four training years is used for fitting; the left-out year is never seen in training and provides the only reported municipal scores for that fold.
Municipal-mean LOO metrics appear in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}; pixel-path LOO metrics and maps appear in Section~\ref{sec:results}.
We do not validate the pixel-level maps against field-level yields because no field-level ground-truth data are available.
