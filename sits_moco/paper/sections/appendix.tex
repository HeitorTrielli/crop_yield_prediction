\section{Pixel-level MSTNet pipeline}
\label{app:pixel-pipeline}

Section~\ref{sec:pixel-formulation} defines the pixel-scoring evaluation~\eqref{eq:pixel-mean-agg}: soy-masked Sentinel-2 pixels are scored one by one, municipal climate is shared across pixels, soil enters by late fusion, and the mean of the pixel predictions is compared with PAM.
Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow} shows the municipality-mean spectral path used for training.
Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow-pixel} shows the pixel-by-pixel path used for the reported LOO metrics and for intramunicipal maps.
At inference the pixel field $\{\hat{y}_p\}$ can be kept as a map or averaged over any polygon. That map is not a training target.

\section{Municipal-mean evaluation on LOO years}
\label{app:muni-mean-loo}

Training encodes soy-masked municipal-mean Sentinel-2 series~\eqref{eq:municipal-mean}.
Table~\ref{tab:loo-metrics} in the main text instead scores every soy pixel and averages the predictions~\eqref{eq:pixel-mean-agg}.
Table~\ref{tab:loo-muni-mean} reports the same left-out years under the municipal-mean evaluation path, using the same fold checkpoints.
The two paths are close on non-drought folds; the 2022 drought fold remains poor under either aggregation ($R^2=-0.300$ municipal-mean versus $-0.422$ pixel-mean).
Excluding 2022, mean municipal-mean LOO $R^2$ is $0.275$.
