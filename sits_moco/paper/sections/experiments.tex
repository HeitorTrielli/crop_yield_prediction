\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We first fix the MSTNet training setup, then define the year leave-one-out design, report municipal-mean LOO metrics, and describe the leave-one-modality ablations.
Section~\ref{sec:results} presents the corresponding pixel-path scores, maps, and ablation tables.

\subsection{Proposed model}
The proposed model is MSTNet: one STNet trunk for the Sentinel-2 series, one for the daily climate series, and late fusion of soil features in the decoder.
Each trunk uses one Transformer encoder layer with $d=128$, four attention heads, feed-forward width $d_{\mathrm{inner}}=64$, and dropout $0.4$.
Optimization uses AdamW with learning rate $1\times10^{-3}$, weight decay $5\times10^{-4}$, and batches of 40 municipalities.
The learning rate drops at epochs 40 and 120; training stops early after fifteen epochs without improvement in the validation loss.
Training and checkpoint selection use the municipal-mean path (Section~\ref{sec:pixel-formulation}).

\subsection{Year leave-one-out design}
\label{sec:loo}
We evaluate temporal generalization with year leave-one-out cross-validation (LOO) over harvest years 2020--2024.
For each fold with left-out year $Y$, all municipality--years with harvest year $\neq Y$ are used for training on the municipal-mean path, and year $Y$ is held out entirely so that no municipality from $Y$ enters training.
Model selection within the fold uses the municipal-mean validation loss on year $Y$; after training, each fold is scored on year $Y$ under both the municipal-mean path and the post-training pixel path.
Input standardization is refit on each fold's training years.
This yields five independently trained checkpoints, one per left-out year.

\subsection{Municipal-mean LOO metrics}
\label{sec:exp-muni-mean}
Table~\ref{tab:loo-muni-mean} reports LOO metrics when the same fold checkpoints are evaluated on the municipal-mean path used in training.
Across the five folds, mean $R^2$ is $0.160$ and mean RMSE is $0.617$\,\si{t/ha}.
Excluding the 2022 drought fold, mean $R^2$ is $0.275$ and mean RMSE is $0.435$\,\si{t/ha}.
On non-drought folds, $R^2$ ranges from $0.199$ in 2023 to $0.410$ in 2021, the strongest year, with RMSE between $0.320$ and $0.577$\,\si{t/ha}.
The 2022 fold collapses to $R^2=-0.300$ and RMSE $=1.342$\,\si{t/ha}, so the five-fold mean is pulled down by that single out-of-distribution season.
Section~\ref{sec:results} reports the corresponding post-training pixel-path scores, maps, and scatterplots.

\subsection{Pixel-path evaluation}
For each LOO fold we also compare municipal predictions with PAM labels on the held-out year under the post-training pixel path~\eqref{eq:pixel-mean-agg}.
The same pixel path is re-run with truncated seasons that keep only the first $k$ months of the Oct--Mar window for $k=1,\ldots,6$, so that early-season $R^2$ can be compared with the full-window score.
Section~\ref{sec:results} reports the full-season per-fold RMSE, MAE, and $R^2$ values, the partial-season $R^2$ curves, predicted-versus-observed scatterplots for every LOO year, municipal error and Mambor\'{e} maps for the best and worst folds, and a 2022 Mambor\'{e} comparison against mean and peak NDVI.
Appendix~\ref{app:muni-mean-eval} reports the municipal-mean counterparts of the partial-season curves, scatterplots, and Paraná error maps; Appendix~\ref{app:extra-heatmaps} adds the remaining pixel-path heatmaps.

\subsection{Modality ablations}
\label{sec:exp-ablations}
To isolate each modality we retrain three ablations on the same year-LOO folds as the full model, each omitting one input stream: no soil (Sentinel-2 + climate), no climate (Sentinel-2 + soil), and no Sentinel-2 (climate + soil).
Hyperparameters match full MSTNet; only the inputs change---late soil fusion is dropped, or the climate trunk is removed, or the Sentinel-2 trunk is removed.
Checkpoint selection still uses municipal-mean validation loss on the held-out year; Section~\ref{sec:ablations} and Appendix~\ref{app:ablations-muni} report pixel-path and municipal-mean scores.
