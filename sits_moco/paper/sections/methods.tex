\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Pixel scoring under municipal supervision}
\label{sec:pixel-formulation}
Let $\mathcal{P}_{m,y}$ denote the soybean pixels observed in municipality $m$ in harvest year $y$.
MSTNet defines a productivity score $\hat{y}_p$ for each soy pixel $p$ from its Sentinel-2 sequence $\mathbf{x}_p$, the municipal daily climate series $\mathbf{c}_{m,y}$ (broadcast to all pixels in $m$), and soil covariates $\mathbf{s}_p$ (or a municipal soil vector when only polygon-level soil is available).
Reported municipal predictions average those pixel scores,
\begin{equation}
  \hat{y}_{m,y}
  =
  \frac{1}{|\mathcal{P}_{m,y}|}
  \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}_{m,y}}
  \hat{y}_p ,
  \qquad
  \hat{y}_p = f_\theta(\mathbf{x}_p, \mathbf{c}_{m,y}, \mathbf{s}_p),
  \label{eq:pixel-mean-agg}
\end{equation}
and are compared with the PAM label $y_{m,y}$.
At inference the field $\{\hat{y}_p\}$ can also be kept as a map or averaged over any sub-municipal polygon.
Appendix~\ref{app:pixel-pipeline} sketches this pipeline.

Training uses a cheaper surrogate: soy-masked Sentinel-2 reflectance is averaged before encoding,
\begin{equation}
  \bar{\mathbf{x}}_{m,y,t}
  =
  \frac{1}{|\mathcal{P}_{m,y,t}|}
  \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}_{m,y,t}}\mathbf{x}_{p,t},
  \label{eq:municipal-mean}
\end{equation}
so each municipality--year is one forward pass
$\hat{y}_{m,y}^{\mathrm{train}}=f_\theta(\bar{\mathbf{x}}_{m,y},\mathbf{c}_{m,y},\mathbf{s}_m)$
supervised by PAM.
The LOO metrics in Sections~\ref{sec:experiments}--\ref{sec:results} are not from that surrogate: they apply the trained weights pixel by pixel and aggregate with~\eqref{eq:pixel-mean-agg}.
Because $f_\theta$ is nonlinear, mean-then-encode and encode-then-mean need not coincide; the pixel-mean evaluation is the operational downscaling check.
Appendix~\ref{app:muni-mean-loo} reports the same LOO years under the municipal-mean evaluation path for reference.

\subsection{Architecture}
MSTNet (\emph{Multi} STNet) places one STNet trunk on each time-varying stream so that modalities with different sampling rates need not share a common calendar~\citep{xu2024sitsmoco}.
A spectral trunk encodes Sentinel-2 sequences (ten bands, length $T=45$) with day-of-year positional encodings and NDVI-weighted temporal pooling.
A climate trunk encodes daily Xavier weather (five channels: precipitation, ETo, Rs, Tmax, Tmin) over the Oct--Mar window (length $183$), pooled by a mean over valid days.
MapBiomas Solo properties are fixed once the harvest year is known. They are concatenated with the pooled trunk embeddings and passed through a multilayer-perceptron decoder that outputs a productivity estimate (per pixel or per municipal aggregate, depending on the input path).
As an ablation on a spectral-only single-trunk STNet, we also try mean pooling over valid spectral timesteps instead of NDVI weights.
That spectral-only model uses ten-band inputs and has no climate or soil branches.

Each MSTNet trunk uses one Transformer layer, $d_{\mathrm{model}}=128$, four attention heads, feed-forward width $d_{\mathrm{inner}}=64$, and dropout $0.4$.

\subsection{Loss and optimization}
Training minimizes mean squared error between predicted and observed municipal productivity after standardizing targets with the training-set mean and standard deviation.
Reported metrics (RMSE, MAE, and $R^2$) are computed after transforming predictions back to \si{t/ha}.

Optimization uses AdamW with learning rate $1\times10^{-3}$, weight decay $5\times10^{-4}$, and batches of 40 municipalities.
The learning rate is scheduled to drop at epochs 40 and 120; training stops early after fifteen epochs without improvement in validation $R^2$.
No self-supervised pretraining is used: the network is trained from scratch under municipal supervision.
