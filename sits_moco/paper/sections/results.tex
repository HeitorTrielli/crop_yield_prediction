\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

We report post-training pixel-path LOO accuracy against PAM, then examine the 2022 drought fold, partial-season truncations, scatterplots and spatial maps, and modality ablations.
Municipal-mean LOO metrics for the same checkpoints are in Section~\ref{sec:exp-muni-mean}; the corresponding municipal-mean scatterplots, error maps, and partial-season curves are in Appendix~\ref{app:muni-mean-eval}.

\subsection{Year-LOO municipal accuracy}
Table~\ref{tab:loo-metrics} reports post-training pixel-path metrics on the left-out year of each fold: every soy pixel is scored and the scores are averaged to the municipality before comparison with PAM.
Deltas are relative to the municipal-mean path on the same checkpoints (Table~\ref{tab:loo-muni-mean}).
Across the five folds, mean $R^2$ is $0.157$ and mean RMSE is $0.623$\,\si{t/ha}.
Excluding the 2022 drought fold, mean $R^2$ rises to $0.302$ and mean RMSE falls to $0.427$\,\si{t/ha}, a mean $\Delta R^2$ of $+0.027$ over the municipal-mean path.
The strongest non-drought fold is 2021 ($R^2=0.402$, RMSE $=0.323$\,\si{t/ha}).

\subsection{Drought year 2022}
Harvest 2022 is the only year in our 2020--2024 sample with a severe drought across Paran\'{a}.
When 2022 is the LOO year, the training set contains no analogous drought season, so the model must extrapolate outside the climatic regimes seen in training.
That fold collapses: $R^2=-0.422$ and RMSE $=1.404$\,\si{t/ha}, far worse than the other LOO years.
Figure~\ref{fig:map-error} places that drought error map beside the best non-drought fold, 2021.
The 2022 panel shows a spatially coherent band of large positive errors across the west and northwest of the state: the model over-predicts where observed PAM productivity collapsed.
That footprint matches the known geography of the 2021/22 drought, which hit the West and Northwest hardest, including the Toledo, Cascavel, and Paranava\'{\i} regions.
Municipalities such as Palotina, Terra Roxa, Medianeira, Toledo, and Assis Chateaubriand appear among the largest over-predictions in our LOO fold.
The remaining folds, trained with 2022 available when another year is held out, do not face that missing-regime problem to the same degree.
We therefore treat 2022 as a stress test of out-of-distribution seasons rather than as a typical LOO score, and we report means both with and without that fold.

\subsection{Partial-season evaluation}
\label{sec:incomplete-series}
After training on the full Oct--Mar window, we also score each LOO fold on truncated inputs that keep only the first $k$ season months, for $k=1,\ldots,6$, on the pixel path.
Table~\ref{tab:incomplete-r2} and Figure~\ref{fig:incomplete-r2} report municipal $R^2$ as months are added.
With one or two months the scores are strongly negative on every fold.
Positive $R^2$ appears around $k=4$ on non-drought years and approaches the full-season value by $k=5$--$6$: excluding 2022, mean $R^2$ rises from $-0.156$ at three months to $0.165$, $0.288$, and $0.302$ at four, five, and six months.
The small gain from adding March, relative to stopping in February, suggests that on the observed years either harvest is largely complete before March begins or almost all information used by the model is already available by late February; we cannot separate these explanations because the actual harvest dates are not observed.
The drought fold stays negative for every truncation.
Appendix~\ref{app:muni-mean-eval} reports the same truncations under the municipal-mean path.

\subsection{Scatterplots and spatial examples}
Figure~\ref{fig:scatter} shows predicted versus observed municipal productivity for every LOO year.
Non-drought folds track the identity line with moderate scatter; the 2022 panel shows the systematic failure under drought.
Even on better years, predictions compress toward a mid-range value: many municipalities are scored near the same average productivity, and the model under-separates the highest-yielding municipalities from the rest.

\subsection{Ablations}
\label{sec:ablations}
Table~\ref{tab:ablations} reports the fixed-architecture leave-one-modality runs from Section~\ref{sec:exp-ablations} on the pixel path; Appendix~\ref{app:ablations-muni} reports the municipal-mean counterparts.
Excluding 2022, full MSTNet has mean $R^2=0.302$ and RMSE $=0.427$\,\si{t/ha}.
Omitting climate costs about $0.32$ in mean $R^2$, omitting Sentinel-2 about $0.26$, and omitting soil about $0.07$.
On the 2022 fold, removing soil lifts $R^2$ from $-0.422$ to $-0.203$, but non-drought years still favor the full three-stream setup, so the five-fold mean does not rise above the full model.
